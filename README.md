CyberGuard is a capstone project developed for the High School and Senior High School
students of the College of Our Lady of Mercy of Pulilan Foundation, Inc. It aims to move away
from traditional teaching methods by using a gamified approach to teach cybersecurity. The
system features episodic modules, interactive minigames, a scoring system, and leaderboards
to make learning about cyber threats (like phishing and malware) engaging and effective.

Key Features:

* Episodic Learning Modules: Story-driven scenarios tackling common cyber threats.

* Interactive Minigames: Decision-based gameplay to test cybersecurity knowledge.  

* Progress Tracking & Leaderboards: Gamification elements to keep students motivated and competitive.  

* Admin Dashboard: For professors to manage classes, track student progress, and monitor overall performance.
